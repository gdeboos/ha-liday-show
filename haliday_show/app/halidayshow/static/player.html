Show player

Leave this page open on a computer or tablet that's connected to your speakers. When a playlist, its schedule or an automation starts a show that has no Show speaker, the song plays here and your lights follow it. Shows you play from the editor, or on a speaker, don't play here.

Waiting for a show. Leave this page open.

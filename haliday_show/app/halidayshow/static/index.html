          Convert to WLED

          For a light now run by a WLED controller. It keeps its name, its tracks in your shows and its place on the house photo. Its parts are stretched to the WLED's number of LEDs.

          Add a speaker

          Press Test and add next to a speaker. It plays a short chime the same way a show starts, and the speaker is added only if that works.

          Some media players, like Echo devices through Alexa Media Player or Emby clients, can't play files from Home Assistant's media folder, so they can't be added.

          Automations

          To play this show from an automation, use the HA-liday Show MQTT entities (pick the show, then press Play), or the hassio.app_stdin action with input {"action": "play", "show": "…"}. The song plays on the show's Show speaker (in the editor's ⚙ Show settings…), or on the Default show speaker above when a show doesn't pick one. The speaker in the Playback panel is only for editing, so you can design on a speaker indoors without changing where the real show plays.

          Pick a light, press Trace and click along it on the photo, starting at the end with the plug.

          Layout

          Each editor has its own layout. Pick one, then its page layout and where each panel sits. It's saved in this browser. You can also drag a panel by its title in the editor.

          Public page

          Lets people passing by start a show from their phone. Turning it on adds a Public page tab, where you choose what they can pick and when.

          AI

          Checking…

          The Describe it panel turns a request in words into effects, using either Claude with your own API key (set it on the app's Configuration tab in Home Assistant), or else Home Assistant's AI Task, which uses whichever AI integration you have set up there, including a local one such as Ollama. It sends the AI your lights' names, the effect list, the song's beats and sections, and the selection; never the song itself.

          Your data

          Everything HA-liday Show saves: your lights, shows, palettes, presets, settings and house photo, in one file. Songs stay in your songs folder and aren't in it.

          Export…

          Before an import or a fresh start, a copy of what you had is kept here, so you can put it back.

          Experimental

          Packet lab

          For trying out commands HA-liday Show doesn't use yet. Pick one of your lights and a starting point, edit the message if you like, and press Send. Anything the light sends back is listed below. A playing show is stopped first. Only lights on the Devices tab can be sent to.

          Reaching past the realtime window

          Some lights, like the 30 m Christmas String Lights 2, only take realtime colours for their first few dozen bulbs. Run sweep tries nine guesses at how the rest might be reached, one after another, each in its own colour, using the size of the window (44 on the H70C5, or Segments above for a light whose window isn't known). Film the whole light while it runs: a colour showing up past the window means that guess works. It takes about 45 seconds.

          A TV sync box does get colour onto the whole of these lights, so it may switch them into a different mode first. Try ways to switch on tries seven guesses at that, one after another, each followed by a rainbow over the window. Watch for the rainbow spreading past the window or along the whole light, and note the number shown below when it does. It takes about 50 seconds.

          Try other flags sends a whole frame of the window in one colour, nine times, each with a different value in the byte that switches crisp or blended colours (only 00, 01 and 02 have been tried). A colour lighting more than the first bulbs means that value reaches further. It takes about 45 seconds.

          Try app modes first switches the light into each of twenty Govee app modes in turn, then sends the usual realtime frame of the window in that step's colour. Between steps the light goes plain white. Watch for a colour reaching past the window, or anything other than the first bulbs lighting up. It takes about a minute and a half.

            
  
            
  
            
    
            Listen for Govee traffic
            
    
            Records the messages your Govee lights send each other that reach Home Assistant, so a way of
      driving them that HA-liday Show doesn't know yet can be worked out. For example, start Scene Stage in the
      Govee Home app, then press Listen. Nothing is sent to your lights, and a playing show is stopped
      first. Afterwards, Download the recording and attach it to a GitHub issue.

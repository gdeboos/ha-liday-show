# Profiles: plans for next year

Keep a plan for next year's display beside the real one, design shows for it, and switch it on when
the new lights go up.

A **profile** holds your house: its own list of lights, house photo and tracing, light groups and light
roles. Everything else is shared by every profile: your songs, shows, scenes, playlists, the schedule
and the visitors' page.

Only one profile is **Live**. That's the one your lights, shows, schedules, Home Assistant and the
visitors' page use. Everyone starts with one profile, **Now**, so nothing changes until you make a
second.

## Make a plan

1. Go to **Setup → Devices** and find **Profiles** near the bottom.
2. Press **+ New profile** and give it a name, such as **Next year**. It starts as a copy of the
   profile you're on: the same lights, photo, tracing, groups and roles.
3. You're now looking at the plan. A banner at the top says so, with **Back to Now**.

In the plan you can:

- add lights you mean to buy, with **+ Add a light → Plan a light**
- take out lights you're planning to replace (they stay in **Now**)
- upload a new house photo and trace the lights on it, under **Setup → House**
- regroup lights, or give them new roles, under **Setup → Devices**

None of this touches tonight's lights. Changing a light that's in both profiles (its name, address or
size, say) changes it in both, because it's the same light. Only its place on the photo can differ.

## Shows in a plan

Shows are shared. Open one while you're looking at a plan, and it has lanes for the plan's lights:
add effects to the new ones as usual. Lanes for lights that aren't in the plan are hidden, never
deleted, so switching back shows them again with their effects.

While you're on a plan, nothing plays on your lights. **▶ Play** plays the show in the editor's preview
only, and **Video…** makes a video of the plan, handy for showing off next year's display. Shows and
scenes you save keep playing as they were on your lights tonight; when you go back to the live profile,
they're saved again for your real lights in the background.

Switch between profiles with **Profile** in the top bar (it appears once you have two), or **Open** beside
each one under **Setup → Devices → Profiles**.

## When the new lights go up

Press **Make this the live one…** beside the plan. After you confirm:

- shows, schedules, Home Assistant and the visitors' page play on its lights and house straight away
- lights that are only in the old profile stop being sent anything
- your shows are saved again for the new lights in the background, which can take a few minutes

The old profile stays as it was, so if the new lights aren't up yet you can make it live again.

Seasons and their **Packed away** switches are shared by every profile, as they're about your real
lights. The **Pre-show check** and **Check all shows** look at the live profile.

## Rename or delete

**Rename…** and **Delete…** sit beside each profile. The live profile can't be deleted. Deleting a
profile deletes its photo, tracing, groups and roles, and any lights that were only in it; your shows
keep their effects.
